A hobby, with a lot of math

Games made to be played.

I'm Kuzzi. I build these games outside of my full-time job because I love incremental games, math, and the moment a complicated problem finally clicks. Mostly, I'm making the games I want to play and sharing them with anyone who enjoys the same kind of puzzle.

This is a passion project, with no investors to answer to and zero pay-to-win. I'd rather give you an interesting decision than sell you the solution.

Every web version is 100% free to play. The full version of Rejected Draft will be sold on Steam; you can try the generously long demo there or play a modified version here for free.
